yukle-py is a private command-line tool. It runs only on its owner's computer and is used only to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's channels as private or scheduled uploads, after the owner reviews and approves each one.
  • Sets thumbnails, caption files and metadata (title, description, tags, category) on the owner's videos.
  • Updates the owner's channel description, keywords and banner.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics for the owner's channels to plan future videos.
